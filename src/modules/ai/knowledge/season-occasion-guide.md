# Season Guide

## Spring
Favor: florals, green/aromatic, light fruity notes. Avoid: heavy oud, thick gourmands.
Rationale: mild temperatures let delicate top and heart notes shine without becoming cloying.

## Summer
Favor: citrus, aquatic/fresh, light aromatic. Avoid: heavy amber, dense woods, thick vanilla.
Rationale: heat amplifies projection — heavy scents can become overwhelming; humidity holds
notes closer to skin, letting lighter families still register.

## Autumn
Favor: warm spices, soft woods, light amber, leather. Avoid: pure aquatic/ozonic (can feel
cold and flat against crisp air). Rationale: cooler air lets warmer, richer notes bloom without
becoming stifling.

## Winter
Favor: amber, oud, gourmand, heavy woods, incense. Avoid: sheer citrus/aquatic alone (fades
fast in cold, dry air and reads as thin). Rationale: cold reduces volatility, so richer, heavier
compositions are needed to project; skin is often covered by layers, so a stronger scent still
reaches others comfortably.

# Occasion Guide

## Office / Professional
Favor: clean, moderate-projection scents — soft woods, light florals, fresh aromatics. Avoid:
loud gourmands, heavy oud, strong leather. Rationale: shared, enclosed spaces require
scent restraint out of courtesy to colleagues.

## Date Night
Favor: warm, slightly sweet or musky compositions — ambers, soft gourmands, skin-musk blends.
Rationale: intimacy calls for a scent that reads well up close, not just from a distance.

## Formal / Wedding / Business Events
Favor: refined, classic families — iris, soft woods, restrained ambers. Avoid: anything overly
trendy, sharp, or polarizing. Rationale: formal settings favor timeless elegance over
statement-making boldness.

## Gym / Daily Casual
Favor: very light, fresh, aquatic or citrus. Avoid: heavy, long-lasting formulas. Rationale:
physical activity increases perceived scent intensity; a light base avoids becoming
overpowering once mixed with sweat.

## Gift for a Partner
Favor: asking about (or inferring) their existing preferences — floral vs. woody vs. sweet —
before defaulting to a "safe" universally-loved bestseller in that family. Rationale: personal
scent preference varies enormously; a well-matched gift signals attentiveness.
