# Perfume Families

## Citrus
Bright, zesty, and instantly energizing. Built on bergamot, lemon, orange, and grapefruit.
Mood: uplifting, clean, sociable. Best worn: daytime, hot climates, office settings, first
dates in warm weather. Typical audience: people who want low-commitment, universally likeable
scents. Performance: light, needs reapplication. Pros: safe, refreshing, unisex. Cons: short
longevity, low projection, common misconception that "citrus = cheap" (many niche houses build
sophisticated citrus compositions).

## Woody
Warm, grounding, built on cedar, sandalwood, vetiver, and patchouli. Mood: confident,
composed, mature. Best worn: autumn/winter, business settings, evenings. Typical audience:
those who want a signature, understated scent. Performance: medium-to-long longevity, moderate
projection. Pros: versatile, ages well on skin. Cons: can skew "safe" if generic; higher-quality
sandalwood/oud accords cost more.

## Oriental / Amber
Rich, resinous, built on vanilla, amber, benzoin, labdanum, and warm spices. Mood: sensual,
luxurious, memorable. Best worn: evenings, cold weather, special occasions. Typical audience:
people who want a statement scent. Performance: long longevity, strong projection. Pros:
distinctive, long-lasting. Cons: can feel heavy in hot weather or overwhelming in close
quarters if over-applied.

## Fresh / Aquatic
Clean, airy, built on marine/ozonic accords, aldehydes, and light musks. Mood: crisp, modern,
minimal. Best worn: summer, gym, casual daytime. Typical audience: those who dislike heavy or
sweet scents. Performance: light-to-medium. Pros: subtle, non-intrusive. Cons: can read as
"generic" if overly simple; lower complexity than other families.

## Floral
Built around rose, jasmine, iris, lily of the valley, tuberose, and ylang-ylang. Mood: romantic,
elegant, soft. Best worn: spring, daytime-to-evening, romantic occasions. Typical audience:
wide — from soft florals to bold "white florals." Performance: varies widely by sub-family.
Pros: broadly appealing, huge stylistic range. Cons: some florals (tuberose, white florals)
can be polarizing or "loud."

## Gourmand
Edible-smelling accords: vanilla, caramel, chocolate, praline, coffee, honey. Mood: cozy,
playful, comforting. Best worn: colder months, casual/date settings. Typical audience: people
who enjoy sweet, dessert-like scents. Performance: long-lasting, high projection. Pros:
comforting and often crowd-pleasing. Cons: can feel juvenile or too sweet if over-applied;
riskier for formal/office settings.

## Leather / Smoky
Built on leather accords, birch tar, tobacco, and smoky woods. Mood: bold, edgy, mature.
Best worn: evenings, cooler weather. Typical audience: fans of distinctive, less-mainstream
scents. Performance: strong, long-lasting. Pros: memorable and rarely generic. Cons: can be
intense for beginners; not ideal for close, scent-sensitive environments like offices.

## Green / Aromatic
Built on herbs (lavender, sage, rosemary), fresh-cut grass, and galbanum. Mood: crisp,
outdoorsy, natural. Best worn: spring/summer daytime, casual settings. Typical audience:
minimalists and fans of "barbershop"-style scents (fougère). Performance: medium. Pros: fresh
without being sweet or aquatic. Cons: can feel dated if built on very classic aromatic accords.
