# Perfume Notes

## Top Notes (first impression, 5–15 minutes)
- **Bergamot**: bright, slightly bitter citrus; the backbone of countless fresh and Eau de
  Cologne-style scents; pairs with lavender, neroli, and woods.
- **Lemon**: sharp, zesty, energizing; short-lived; common in summer and unisex scents.
- **Pink Pepper**: sparkling, slightly fruity-spicy; adds lift to florals and woods without
  heaviness; very popular in modern niche releases.
- **Grapefruit**: tart, slightly bitter, modern; frequently paired with vetiver and ginger.

## Middle / Heart Notes (the character, 20 minutes–2 hours in)
- **Rose**: the most versatile floral note; ranges from soft/powdery to jammy/fruity depending
  on the variety used; pairs with oud, patchouli, saffron, and musks.
- **Jasmine**: heady, rich, slightly animalic; central to most "white floral" compositions;
  pairs beautifully with sandalwood and vanilla.
- **Lavender**: herbal-floral, calming, classic in fougère and barbershop scents; pairs with
  bergamot, tonka bean, and geranium.
- **Iris (Orris)**: powdery, cool, elegant, and expensive to produce naturally; signature of
  refined, understated florals.
- **Cinnamon / Cardamom**: warm spice notes that add depth to orientals and gourmands without
  necessarily reading as sweet.

## Base Notes (the lasting foundation, hours later)
- **Sandalwood**: creamy, soft, milky wood; excellent longevity anchor; pairs with almost
  everything.
- **Vetiver**: earthy, smoky-green root note; a backbone of many masculine and unisex
  compositions.
- **Patchouli**: earthy, slightly sweet, sometimes chocolatey; central to chypre and modern
  gourmand-woody blends.
- **Vanilla**: warm, sweet, comforting; ranges from gourmand-sweet to boozy/resinous depending
  on the extraction and pairing.
- **Tonka Bean**: warm, almond-like, slightly hay-like sweetness; common in modern ambers.
- **Amber**: a blended accord (labdanum, benzoin, vanilla) rather than a single raw material;
  reads as warm, resinous, and cozy.
- **Musk**: soft, skin-like, "clean laundry" character; used to extend longevity and soften
  sharper notes.
- **Oud (Agarwood)**: dark, smoky, medicinal-woody, extremely potent; central to modern Middle
  Eastern-influenced fragrances; a small amount goes a long way.
- **Leather**: smoky, slightly sharp, sometimes birch-tar-based; associated with confident,
  mature compositions.
- **Incense**: smoky, resinous, spiritual; common in unisex and "cool weather" scents.

## General Rules of Thumb
- Citrus and light fruits = top notes, fade fastest.
- Florals and spices = heart notes, define the scent's personality.
- Woods, musks, resins = base notes, drive longevity and depth.
- Higher base-note concentration generally means longer wear but slower initial "reveal."
